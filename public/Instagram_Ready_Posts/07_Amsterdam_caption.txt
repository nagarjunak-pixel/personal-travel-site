A city built on water, wheels and tolerance. 🚲 Amsterdam has more bikes than people and a ring of 17th-century canals — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — lined with narrow, leaning merchant houses. It's also a city of giants: Rembrandt and Van Gogh both have world-class museums here. Much of it sits below sea level, reclaimed from the water by centuries of Dutch engineering.
