One of the most beautiful cities on the planet, wrapped around a flat-topped mountain. ⛰️ Cape Town sits where two oceans meet, beneath the iconic Table Mountain (one of the New 7 Wonders of Nature). Penguins on the beaches, vineyards in the hills, and the Cape of Good Hope where ships once braved the journey between worlds. Raw natural drama in every direction.
