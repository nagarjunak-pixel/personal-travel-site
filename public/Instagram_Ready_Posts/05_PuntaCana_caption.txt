50 km of palm-lined white sand on the Caribbean's edge. 🌴 Punta Cana is the Dominican Republic's beach paradise, where the Atlantic and Caribbean meet. Beyond the resorts, this is the island Columbus landed on in 1492 — the oldest European settlement in the Americas is just down the coast. Merengue and bachata were born here; the rhythm is everywhere.
