The city of Nawabs, where courtesy is an art form. 🕌 Lucknow is the heart of Awadhi culture — grand monuments like the Bara Imambara (with its mind-bending maze, the Bhulbhulaiya), refined Urdu poetry, intricate chikankari embroidery, and some of the most famous food in India (kebabs and biryani that melt on the tongue). Old-world elegance, still alive.

📍 Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India
