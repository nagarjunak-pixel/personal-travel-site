Bhutan doesn't measure success in GDP — it measures Gross National Happiness. 🇧🇹 The Ha and Paro valleys are home to cliff-clinging monasteries, prayer flags strung across pine ridges, and air so clean it feels like another century. This is also the only carbon-negative country on Earth — it absorbs more CO₂ than it emits. The last Himalayan kingdom, on its own terms.
